## Open the settings

- `piwi.failureSeverity`: how a CI failure shows in the Problems panel, `error`, `warning`, `information`, or `off`.
- `piwi.statusBarAlert`: which failures turn the status bar item red, `new` ones from your runs, `any` or `never`.
- `piwi.runNotifications`: when a run started from the editor says what it changed, `always`, `failures` or `never`.
- `piwi.hiddenMessages`: the messages a **Don't Show Again** hid; remove one to see it again.
- `piwi.breakpoints`: whether the runs Piwi starts pause at your breakpoints, in the browser.
- `piwi.serverUrl` and `piwi.project`: the instance and the project, which **Piwi: Connect…** sets.
- `piwi.trace.server`: writes the traffic with the editor service to the log, which **Piwi: Open the log** shows.

The colors Piwi draws with, such as `piwi.failingTestBackground`, change in `workbench.colorCustomizations`.

**Piwi: Get started** opens this walkthrough again.
